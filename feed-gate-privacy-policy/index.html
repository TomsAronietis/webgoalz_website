Feed Gate Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026 · Published by WebGoalz (webgoalz.com)

What is Feed Gate?

Feed Gate is a Chrome extension that blocks distractions, lust and laziness, so you can use the internet for work without getting pulled into it.

  • Algorithmic feeds: it hides the endless feeds on Instagram (home feed, Reels, Explore), YouTube (homepage, Shorts, Trending), TikTok (For You, Explore, Live) and Facebook (News Feed, Reels, Watch).
  • Adult content: it blocks adult websites, stops adult searches on any site and keeps SafeSearch switched on.
  • Off-work searches: it stops searches for games, anime, movies and similar during work.

What you actually need keeps working: search, profiles, single posts and videos, and messages. It is built by WebGoalz for creators, freelancers and anyone who wants to stay focused, and it can also be locked on a computer by a parent or accountability partner.

Your privacy in short

Feed Gate works inside your browser. We (WebGoalz) never receive, store or sell any of your data. We run no servers, analytics or ads. Data leaves your device only in two cases: when you turn on the optional AI check with your own Google Gemini API key, and when Feed Gate's own pages load their fonts from Google Fonts.

This policy explains what the Feed Gate Chrome extension (“Feed Gate”, “the extension”) does with information on your device. It applies to the extension only.

Information Feed Gate reads on your device

To do its job, Feed Gate reads the following on your device only. None of it is sent to WebGoalz.

  • Web page addresses (URLs). Feed Gate checks the address of the page you're on to recognise feed pages and to find the search term in search result addresses, such as ?q= on Google.
  • Text typed into search boxes. When you press Enter in a search box, or pause while typing in one, Feed Gate checks the text against its word lists.

Feed Gate does not read the text, images or videos on the pages you visit. It does not read your emails, messages, passwords or form entries outside search boxes.

Information Feed Gate stores

WhatWhere it is kept
Your settings: which sites and filters are on, your word lists and extra sites to blockChrome's extension storage. If you have Chrome Sync turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own signed-in browsers through your Google account.
Your Gemini API key, if you enter oneChrome's local extension storage on this device only. It is never synced.
AI check results: a search and whether it was judged adult, off-work or fineChrome's local extension storage on this device only, up to the most recent 3,000 searches. This is so the same search is never sent twice.

Information that leaves your device

1. The optional AI check (Google Gemini). The AI check is only active if you, or an administrator of your computer, add a Google Gemini API key. When it is active, and a search is not already caught by the word lists, Feed Gate sends Google:

  • the search text (in lower case, up to 200 characters), and
  • your API key, which Google needs to accept the request.

Google classifies the search and sends back a one-word answer. Each search is sent at most once; after that the stored answer is used. This request goes directly from your browser to Google, never through WebGoalz. Google's handling of it is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. Depending on the plan your API key belongs to, Google may use the content of requests to improve its products. Leave the key field empty to switch the AI check off completely.

2. Fonts. Feed Gate's settings page and its “This site is blocked” page load the Inter and Poppins fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google receives your IP address and browser details. No search terms or settings are included. See Google Fonts privacy.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect, receive or store your data on any server. WebGoalz has no access to it.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it with anyone for advertising.
  • We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising code.
  • We do not use any data for anything other than blocking the content you asked Feed Gate to block.

The use of information received by Feed Gate adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Computers managed by an administrator

A parent, employer or other administrator of your computer can install Feed Gate through Chrome policy and set its settings, including the word lists and a Gemini API key. Those settings are stored by Chrome and Windows on that computer, and you cannot change them from the extension. WebGoalz does not receive them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Feed Gate stores stays until you change it or remove the extension. Removing Feed Gate from Chrome deletes its stored settings, API key and AI check results from your device. You can change your settings, word lists and API key at any time on Feed Gate's options page. To delete the searches Google has received, see your Google account and the Gemini API terms.

Children

Feed Gate is a content filter and may be installed by parents on a child's computer. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Feed Gate starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.
